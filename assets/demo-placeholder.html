To add your demo video, upload assets/demo.mp4 and edit the project card in index.html to include the video tag below:

<video controls muted playsinline width="560">
  <source src="assets/demo.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

Alternatively, point the source src to a public URL (YouTube embed is also possible but I recommend a small MP4 for offline reliability).
